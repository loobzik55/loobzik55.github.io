Research from my internship at Knick Interface LLC, summer 2026. AI racks are moving facility power from AC to 800 VDC, and the move breaks how current gets measured.

Technical articleAug 2026Shunt · Hall · Fluxgate

Measuring current in the 800 VDC data center

Current transformers and Rogowski coils need changing flux, so they read nothing on a DC bus. Hall effect is the only AC sensor that survives, and DC is exactly where it performs worst.

On AC, a Hall sensor's offset sits at 0 Hz and the signal doesn't, so a filter removes the error for free. On DC both sit at 0 Hz, and re-zeroing needs zero current, which means an outage. A fault also saturates the core and leaves a permanent remanence offset.

F2 · CB-02 · ANSI 50/51 overcurrent relay · protection

Standards and five nines

A companion memo mapping which IEC, UL, IEEE, ISO and EU rules apply to current measurement in a DC data center, and what the 99.999% uptime target means for sensor choice.

Standards memoAug 2026IEC · UL · IEEE · ISO · EU

Five nines at 3 kA/µs

No standard in force today governs current measurement inside an 800 VDC data center. The rules that will govern it already exist in HVDC, photovoltaics, EV and telecom DC plant.

  1. There is no DC data center current-measurement standard.IEC 61869-14, the DC current transformer standard, starts above 1.5 kV. An 800 VDC rack is below its floor.
  2. The binding accuracy number comes from metering.ISO/IEC 30134-2 sets where PUE is measured but no tolerance. DC accuracy classes live in IEC 62053-41, and (EU) 2024/1364 makes error a reporting issue for rooms ≥ 500 kW.
  3. Protection sets the bandwidth requirement.Transients can exceed 3 kA/µs and solid-state breakers turn off in about 420 ns. A 100 kHz sensor lets roughly 10,500 A through before it settles.
  4. "Five nines" is a procurement expectation.TIA-942, EN 50600 / ISO/IEC 22237 and BICSI 002 classify by topology, not by an availability percentage.
  5. Failure mode matters more than failure rate.A sensor bolted into the conductor joins the series availability chain. One that reads the conductor without joining it does not.

F3 · CB-03 · Li-ion cell · first-order RC model

Battery charging model

MathWorks Classroom Challenge, June–August 2026. I led a four-person team through "Modeling and Analyzing a Battery Charging Profile," assigning data setup, model fitting, analysis and visualization by each member's strengths.

Team leadMATLAB Live ScriptCurve Fitting Toolbox
V(t) = Vmax(1 − e−t/RC)

The cell's charging voltage treated as a first-order RC circuit. R and C come from a fit to MathWorks' Li-ion dataset; their product τ sets how fast the cell approaches full voltage.

  1. Isolated one charge cycle's time, voltage, current and power, and recorded Vmax.
  2. Fit the RC equation to extract R and C, and checked the fit with R².
  3. Plotted V, I and P as stacked subplots with the fit over the data.
  4. Computed dV/dt with gradient, energy with trapz, and the 80% and 100% timestamps.
  5. Estimated I²R heating losses from the fitted R.
  6. Cross-checked numerical against analytical derivatives.
